Questions

Quick answers.

Is this generative AI video?

No. VidToon Studio animates ready-made cartoon characters from your script. It is not a generative model like Veo or Runway, so it is fast and completely free.

Does the downloaded video include the voice?

Browsers cannot record their built-in text-to-speech, so the download has no synthetic voice. You can upload your own voiceover or music in step 5, or add a voice afterwards in a free editor like CapCut.

Which file format do I get?

WebM on Chrome and Android, MP4 on browsers that support it. Both work on YouTube, and CapCut can convert them.

Are my scripts saved?

No. Everything happens inside your browser and nothing is sent to a server.
